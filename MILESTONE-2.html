The goal

From the roadmap: machine learning stops being metaphor. Write PyTorch and say what every line does. TrenTorch is the spine. Five weeks of session A give about 12.5 hours, so this block covers TrenTorch modules 01 to 08, from tensors to the training loop, and ends with a small network trained from scratch and explained line by line.
